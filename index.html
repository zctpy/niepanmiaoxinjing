涅槃妙心经

序品第一 · Chapter One: The Opening Chapter
如是我闻一时佛在大灵鹫山
灵鹫山大众云集

如是我闻。

Thus have I heard.

一时佛在大灵鹫山。

At one time, the Buddha was dwelling on Great Vulture Peak.

与大比丘八万人俱。

He was accompanied by eighty thousand great bhikkhus.

尊者阿难、尊者舍利弗、尊者摩诃目连、尊者摩诃迦叶，而为上首。

The Venerable Ananda, the Venerable Shariputra, the Venerable Mahamaudgalyayana, and the Venerable Mahakashyapa were among their foremost leaders.

与菩萨众八万人俱。

He was also accompanied by eighty thousand bodhisattvas.

观世音菩萨、阿逸陀菩萨、行愿普贤菩萨、文殊师利菩萨，而为上首。

Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, Ajita Bodhisattva, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva of Great Vows and Practice, and Manjushri Bodhisattva were among their foremost leaders.

诸大梵天王、释提桓因、毗沙门王、大持国王，无量天众俱。

The great Brahma kings, Shakra, King Vaishravana, King Dhritarashtra, and countless celestial beings were also present.

海龙王、夜叉王、阿修罗王，无量神众俱。

The Dragon Kings of the seas, the Yaksha kings, the Asura kings, and countless spiritual beings were assembled there.

阿闍世王、波斯匿王、宰官婆罗门，无量人众俱。

King Ajatashatru, King Prasenajit, government officials, Brahmins, and countless human beings were also present.

达多地狱、焰口饿鬼、金色师子、六牙象王，无量非人俱。

Beings from the Datta Hell, flame-mouthed hungry ghosts, golden lions, six-tusked elephant kings, and countless nonhuman beings were also present.

各礼佛足，退坐一面。

They all bowed at the Buddha's feet, withdrew, and took their seats to one side.

东行震旦 · 五宝台三王

尔时世尊，从多宝佛入塔下，大众围绕，飞行东方百千里程。

At that time, the World-Honored One descended from the stupa of Many Treasures Buddha, surrounded by the assembly, and traveled through the air hundreds of thousands of miles toward the east.

有一国土名大七宝震旦。

There was a land called Great Seven-Treasure Zhendan.

其国中央有一高山，名五宝台。

At the center of that land stood a lofty mountain called the Five-Treasure Terrace.

文殊师利常在说法。

Manjushri Bodhisattva frequently resided there, expounding the Dharma.

世尊至于此山，告大众言。

When the World-Honored One arrived at this mountain, he addressed the assembly, saying:

昔于此国，观世音菩萨化作三禅初梵王，化人间故，没梵宫殿，下生王宫。

Long ago, in this land, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva manifested as the Great Brahma King of the First Heaven of the Third Dhyana, descending from the Brahma palace and taking birth in a royal palace to transform the human world.

大德龙身，说示天理五行气变化吉凶法。

Manifesting a noble dragon-like form, this being taught the principles of Heaven, the Five Phases, the transformations of vital energy, and the patterns associated with fortune and misfortune.

地没还天，作春天主。

After disappearing into the earth, he returned to Heaven and became the presiding lord of spring.

观世音菩萨化作初禅二大梵王，化人间故，没天宫殿，下生王宫。

Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva also manifested as the Second Great Brahma King of the First Dhyana, descending from the celestial palace and taking birth in a royal palace to transform the human world.

火德牛头，说示地利百药医毒助人间气。

With the virtue of fire and a bull-headed form, this being taught the benefits of the earth, the healing properties and toxic effects of hundreds of medicines, and methods of assisting the vital energy of humankind.

地没还天，作夏天主。

After disappearing into the earth, he returned to Heaven and became the presiding lord of summer.

观世音菩萨化作帝释，化人间故，没天宫殿，下生王宫。

Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva also manifested as Emperor Shakra, descending from the celestial palace and taking birth in a royal palace to transform the human world.

土德人质，说示人伦心法大道身法五伦。

With the virtue of earth and a human form, this being taught the principles governing human relationships, the great Way of the mind, the cultivation of the body, and the Five Human Relationships.

地没还天，作土用主。

After disappearing into the earth, he returned to Heaven and became the presiding lord of the earth element.

是三王者，说示天地人三才性德。

These three kings revealed the inherent virtues and principles of Heaven, Earth, and Humanity—the Three Powers.

依俗说法，示第一义。

Using teachings suited to worldly understanding, they revealed the Supreme Truth.

皆是观音大悲应化故。

All these manifestations arose from the great compassion of Avalokiteshvara.

此国观音有缘。

The beings of this land have a karmic affinity with Avalokiteshvara.

末法世中，我法流布。

In the age of the decline of the Dharma, my teaching will spread throughout this land.

余佛缘灭，唯存观音，化益永世。

When the karmic connections with other Buddhas have faded, the connection with Avalokiteshvara alone will remain, bringing transformation and benefit to the world throughout the ages.

说是语已，三王出现，顶礼佛足。

Having spoken these words, the three kings appeared and bowed at the Buddha's feet.

而白佛言，世尊，如今佛说如是。

They addressed the Buddha, saying, “World-Honored One, what the Buddha has just proclaimed is indeed so.”

大日发国 · 天女日月

尔时世尊，从座而起，为三王及大众等，亦行东方万里海上。

At that time, the World-Honored One rose from his seat and, accompanied by the three kings and the great assembly, traveled eastward for thousands of miles across the sea.

海中有国名大日发，东西南北围绕诸眷属国。

In the midst of the sea lay a land called Great Sun Emergence, surrounded in every direction by its affiliated lands.

世尊至于此国海边，告大众言。

When the World-Honored One arrived at the coast of this land, he addressed the assembly, saying:

往昔劫初，观世音菩萨化作色界第十梵王。

At the beginning of a past kalpa, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva manifested as the Tenth Brahma King of the Form Realm.

告初梵王，汝下空中欲界天界，次第造天地。

He instructed the First Brahma King, saying, “Descend through the heavens of the Desire Realm and, in due order, establish Heaven and Earth.”

最后造斯国及眷属海中国山河草木。

“Finally, establish this land and its affiliated islands in the sea, together with their mountains, rivers, and vegetation.”

时有一天女，名妙辨才。

At that time, there was a celestial maiden named Wonderful Eloquence.

是亦观世音菩萨无量劫来以女像作大福田，来为王女。

She too was Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, who through countless kalpas had manifested in female form as a great field of merit and now appeared as a king's daughter.

即成日轮及月光轮，主四天下。

She then brought forth the solar orb and the lunar orb, presiding over the Four Continents of the world.

眷属无量住此国中，或主或伴。

Countless affiliated beings dwelt in this land, some as leaders and others as companions.

是故其国观音有缘。

Therefore, the beings of this land have a karmic affinity with Avalokiteshvara.

末法世中，我法流布。

In the age of the decline of the Dharma, my teaching will spread throughout this land.

余佛缘灭，唯存观音，化益永世。

When the karmic connections with other Buddhas have faded, the connection with Avalokiteshvara alone will remain, bringing transformation and benefit to the world throughout the ages.

说是语已，天女出现，顶礼佛足。

Having spoken these words, the celestial maiden appeared and bowed at the Buddha's feet.

而白佛言，世尊，如今佛说如是。

She addressed the Buddha, saying, “World-Honored One, what the Buddha has just proclaimed is indeed so.”

海中诸国土 · 结缘成佛

尔时世尊，从座而起，为天女及大众等，亦行东方。

At that time, the World-Honored One rose from his seat and, accompanied by the celestial maiden and the great assembly, continued traveling eastward.

于其海中有诸国土。

Within that sea lay numerous lands.

世尊至于此国海边，告大众言。

When the World-Honored One arrived at the coast of one such land, he addressed the assembly, saying:

此国众生质近畜生。

The beings of this land are deeply immersed in dispositions resembling those of animals.

虽我正法渐渐流布，难有悟入。

Although my true Dharma will gradually spread among them, few will find it easy to awaken to and enter its profound meaning.

但作结缘，为成佛缘。

For now, it is enough to establish a karmic connection with the Dharma, thereby planting the seeds of future Buddhahood.

还山付嘱 · 妙法莲华

尔时世尊，为诸大众，还至本处灵鹫山顶，敷座而坐。

At that time, the World-Honored One returned with the great assembly to his original place atop Vulture Peak, spread his seat, and sat down.

告诸大众，我不久当般涅槃。

He addressed the assembly, saying, “Before long, I shall enter Parinirvana.”

诸大众意有欲问法，自恣为问。

“If any among you wish to ask about the Dharma, please feel free to ask.”

时诸大众，默然而坐，一切无声。

At that moment, the entire assembly sat in silence, and not a sound was heard.

尔时世尊，非现神而示妙相，成就妙大方广佛心真如莲华。

At that time, the World-Honored One did not display supernatural powers in the ordinary sense, but revealed a wondrous aspect: the sublime, vast, and all-encompassing Lotus of Suchness, the true nature of the Buddha-mind.

成就五百那由他劫前受用法身。

He revealed the Sambhogakaya, the Enjoyment Body of the Dharma, cultivated and perfected through five hundred nayutas of kalpas in the past.

成就佛佛能尽究竟诸法实相。

He revealed the ultimate capacity of every Buddha to fully comprehend the true reality of all phenomena.

成就华严阿含方等般若一乘修多罗藏。

He revealed the complete treasury of the One Vehicle sutras, encompassing the Avatamsaka, Agama, Vaipulya, and Prajna teachings.

成就妙法莲华光明真如大愿。

He revealed the great vow of luminous Suchness embodied in the wondrous Lotus of the True Dharma.

成就过去无量劫前入灭不灭。

He revealed the mystery of entering Nirvana in the distant past without ever truly ceasing to be.

成就宝塔，是法界塔，常寂光土。

He revealed the jeweled stupa as the Stupa of the Dharma Realm itself, abiding in the Land of Eternal Stillness and Light.

成就分座，是即理智自他法身。

He revealed the sharing of the seat as the unity of principle and wisdom, of self and other, and of the Dharma Body.

如是成就二躬世尊牟尼如来，化现灵鹫华藏世界。

Thus the two World-Honored Ones, the Sage of the Shakyas and the Tathagata of Many Treasures, manifested the wondrous world of the Lotus Treasury at Vulture Peak.

多宝如来已还宝座。

Many Treasures Tathagata had returned to his jeweled seat.

成就寂光世界。

The Land of Eternal Stillness and Light was thereby revealed.

二土融通，成就一土。

The two lands merged and interpenetrated, becoming one single realm.

是即开见，凡夫心中，元有佛心。

This was the opening of vision through which it became clear that, within the minds of ordinary beings, the Buddha-mind has always been present.

凡夫成佛，至大妙法。

For ordinary beings to realize Buddhahood is the supreme and wondrous Dharma.

是即欲以此妙法藏，而付后师摩诃迦叶所由相也。

This was the very sign that the treasury of this wondrous Dharma was about to be entrusted to the future patriarch, Mahakashyapa.

尔时世尊，不起法华妙道法座。

At that time, the World-Honored One remained seated upon the wondrous Dharma seat of the Lotus Way, without rising from it.

凡夫成佛 · 至大妙法
For ordinary beings to realize Buddhahood is the supreme and wondrous Dharma.
