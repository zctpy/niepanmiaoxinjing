佛教智慧双语Bilingual Version of Buddhist Wisdom
涅槃妙心经（又名大梵天王问佛决疑经）The Wonderful Nirvana Mind Sutra (also known as The Sutra in Which Brahma the Great King Questions the Buddha for Resolving Doubts)
这本经典太重要，以至于被魔障的很厉害，久久不得流通，所幸我无意间找到了网上实体书，购买回来之后如获至宝，自己研究了一年，手抄了一个多月，拍照上传给AI转为简体字，又用CHATGPT和豆包一起制作成为双语文章，希望有缘众生能够读到，开卷有益。This scripture is of such great importance that it has long been obstructed by demonic hindrances and kept from widespread circulation. Fortunately, I chanced upon a physical copy online. After purchasing it, I treasured it as a priceless gem. I studied it by myself for a full year, hand-copied it for more than a month, uploaded photos of the manuscript to an AI tool to convert the text into Simplified Chinese. Then I worked with ChatGPT and Doubao to produce this bilingual text. May all sentient beings with karmic affinity encounter it and gain blessings and insight upon reading
